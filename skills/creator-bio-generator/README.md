# Creator Bio Generator — Quick Start Guide

## What Is This?

The Creator Bio Generator turns Claude (or ChatGPT) into a creator branding specialist. Give it your niche, your personality, and a few credentials — and it writes platform-native bios for every channel you use.

Most creators have the same problem: they write one bio and paste a variation of it everywhere. It doesn't work. Instagram bios are read on a 4-inch screen in 3 seconds. LinkedIn summaries are scanned by brand managers who want to know if you're worth a partnership email. TikTok bios need to communicate your whole vibe in one punchy line. The Creator Bio Generator knows the difference.

## How to Install

### Claude Projects
1. Open your Claude project (or create a new one for your creator brand)
2. Go to **Project Knowledge** in the left sidebar
3. Upload `SKILL.md` into the knowledge base
4. Start a new conversation — Claude is now your bio specialist

### ChatGPT
1. Open ChatGPT and go to **Explore GPTs** → **Create**
2. Paste the contents of `SKILL.md` into the **Instructions** field
3. Save your GPT and start generating bios

## Your First 5 Minutes

Once installed, try one of these prompts:

### All Platforms At Once
```
I make calisthenics videos for skinny beginners. I'm at 50K YouTube subscribers,
been posting for 3 years, and my vibe is direct and no-nonsense — no hype, just
what actually works. I use Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, LinkedIn, and YouTube.
Write all my bios.
```

### Quick Single Platform
```
Write me an Instagram bio. I'm a productivity creator targeting remote workers.
Around 10K followers across platforms. Tone is practical, slightly dry humor.
```

### Refresh an Existing Bio
```
Here's my current Instagram bio: [paste it]
I'm a [niche] creator with [X] subscribers. Rewrite this to not sound like
every other creator bio out there.
```

### Specific Audience Focus
```
I review productivity apps for remote workers and freelancers. 10K YouTube subs.
Dry sense of humor. Write my TikTok and Twitter bios only.
```

## Tips for Best Results

- **Be specific about your audience.** "Fitness creator" gives mediocre bios. "Calisthenics for skinny guys aged 18-25 who've never touched a barbell" gives bios that actually resonate with the right people — and repel the wrong ones.
- **Share one real result.** Subscriber count, a transformation story, years in your niche, a notable video that blew up — any concrete detail makes bios dramatically more credible than vague descriptions.
- **Describe your personality honestly.** The skill writes differently for a "dry, analytical, no-hype" creator than for a "loud, energetic, hype-you-up" creator. Tell it your vibe, even roughly.
- **Let it generate all platforms first, then refine.** Ask for everything, then tell it which bios to adjust. It's easier to react to something than to spec it from scratch.
- **Read the Consistency Notes section.** This tells you the brand thread running through all your bios. If something feels off, the notes will help you articulate what to change.
