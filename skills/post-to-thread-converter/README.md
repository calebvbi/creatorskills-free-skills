# Post-to-Thread Converter — Quick Start Guide

## What Is This?

The Post-to-Thread Converter turns Claude (or ChatGPT) into a social media repurposing specialist. Give it any long-form content — a blog post, YouTube transcript, newsletter, or even rough notes — and it outputs two ready-to-post formats: a Twitter/X thread with a real narrative arc, and a LinkedIn post that actually sounds like a person wrote it.

Two formats. One input. No rewriting from scratch.

## How to Install

### Claude Projects
1. Open your Claude project (or create a new one at claude.ai)
2. Go to **Project Knowledge** in the left sidebar
3. Upload `SKILL.md` into the knowledge base
4. Start a new conversation — Claude is now your repurposing specialist

### ChatGPT
1. Open ChatGPT and go to **Explore GPTs** > **Create**
2. Paste the contents of `SKILL.md` into the **Instructions** field
3. Save your GPT and start converting content

## Your First 5 Minutes

Once installed, paste in your content and try one of these prompts:

### Paste the full thing
```
Here's a blog post I wrote. Convert it to a Twitter thread and LinkedIn post:

[paste your content here]
```

### With voice context
```
Here's a newsletter I wrote — my voice is casual and direct, not
corporate. Convert it to a Twitter thread and LinkedIn post:

[paste your content here]
```

### From a rough brain dump
```
I have rough notes from a video I just filmed. They're messy but
the core idea is good. Turn this into a thread and LinkedIn post:

[paste your notes here]
```

### From a transcript
```
Here's the transcript from my YouTube video about [topic].
Pull out the best angle and convert it to a thread and LinkedIn post:

[paste transcript here]
```

## Tips for Best Results

- **Give it the full thing.** The skill is designed to find the best angle in your content — it can't do that if you summarize it first. Paste the full blog post or transcript, not a condensed version.
- **Add voice context if it helps.** A quick note like "my writing style is casual and a bit sarcastic" or "I write for a technical audience" will make the output sharper. One sentence is enough.
- **Don't pre-edit for the AI.** If your original has a great personal story buried in paragraph seven, the skill will find it. You don't need to pull it out first.
- **Iterate on the thread hook.** The hook tweet is the most important part. If the first version doesn't feel right, ask for three alternative hook options. Pick the one that makes you stop.
- **Check character counts before posting.** Tweets are hard-capped at 280 characters. The skill tries to stay under, but always verify before you paste into Twitter.
