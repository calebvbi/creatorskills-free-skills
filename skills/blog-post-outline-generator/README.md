# Blog Post Outline Generator

## What it does

This free skill turns a rough topic or keyword into a complete blog post outline you can draft from immediately. It gives you title options, H2/H3 structure, proof ideas, internal link anchors, and CTA direction.

It is built for creators who want structure first, then writing momentum.

## Who it's for

- Newsletter writers expanding into SEO content
- Creators publishing educational blog posts
- Freelancers and solo teams who need faster content planning

## Key features

- Search-intent-aware outline structure
- 3 title options with keyword fit
- 4 to 6 H2 sections with detailed H3 talking points
- Built-in proof ideas and CTA planning
- Natural upgrade path to conversion-focused writing

## Example use case

You know your topic but keep delaying the draft because the structure feels unclear. Paste your keyword and audience. You get a full outline in minutes, then write section by section without overthinking.

## Starter prompts

- `Generate a blog outline for "blog post template AI" aimed at YouTubers building email lists.`
- `I need an outline for "AI tools for podcast creators" with an informational search intent.`
- `Create a commercial-style blog outline for "best short-form video workflow" with CTA to book a strategy call.`

## How to install

1. Upload `SKILL.md` into your Claude Project or custom GPT instructions.
2. Start with one starter prompt.
3. Copy the outline into your writing doc and draft section by section.
