## What Is This?

The KDP Self-Publishing Launch Kit takes you from book idea to published product. It doesn't just help you write — it helps you launch.

What you get:
- Niche validation and competitive analysis before you write a word
- Optimized title, subtitle, keywords, and descriptions
- Chapter outline built around reader psychology
- Designer-ready cover brief with genre conventions
- 21-day launch calendar
- Amazon ad starter framework
- Pricing strategy calibrated to your category

Best for:
- YouTubers, podcasters, and bloggers with audience expertise to package as a book
- Creators looking for an additional revenue stream that compounds
- Anyone who tried self-publishing before and "uploaded and hoped"

2. Paste `SKILL.md` into Instructions

## Example Use Case

**Scenario:** You've run a beginner fitness YouTube channel for 3 years. 80K subscribers, 15K email list. You want to publish a book but the process feels overwhelming.

**What you do:** Open the Launch Kit. Tell it your topic (home gym on a budget), audience (beginners, 22-35, apartment dwellers), and platform (YouTube + newsletter).

**What you get:** A validation report showing "home gym on a budget" has 12K monthly Amazon searches with moderate competition. A title recommendation front-loading "budget" and "apartment." An outline answering the #1 beginner question first. A cover brief specifying bold sans-serif, clean apartment corner photo, blue-orange color scheme. And a 21-day calendar.

**Result:** Top 5 in category in week one. 400 email list copies in 48 hours. A new evergreen revenue stream.
