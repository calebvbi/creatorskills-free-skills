# KDP Self-Publishing Launch Kit — Worked Example

## Scenario

> "I've been running a fitness YouTube channel for 3 years. I want to write a short ebook about building a home gym on a budget. Audience is beginners intimidated by expensive equipment. 15K newsletter subscribers. Never published a book before."

## Full Output

### Step 1 — Niche & Keyword Validation

**Category:** Health > Exercise > Home Workouts

**Top 10 Competitive Audit:**
| Book | Reviews | Price | Pages | Gap |
|------|---------|-------|-------|-----|
| Home Gym Bible | 312 | $9.99 | 280 | Too long, assumes space |
| Budget Fitness | 89 | $4.99 | 150 | Generic, no apartment focus |
| Small Space Strength | 45 | $6.99 | 120 | Good, but no budget breakdown |

**Demand Signals:**
- Amazon search: "home gym budget" → 1,200 monthly searches
- "apartment workout equipment" → rising trend
- "small space fitness" → consistent 800/month

**Differentiation Angle:** The only book that maps exact equipment to exact apartment constraints with a $200 total budget.

**Estimated Monthly Revenue:** 150 sales/month × $4.99 × 70% = ~$525/month at steady state

---

### Step 2 — Metadata

**Title:** The $200 Home Gym

**Subtitle:** A Step-by-Step Guide to Building a Complete Workout Setup in Any Apartment — No Drills, No Clutter, No Excuses

**7 Backend Keywords:**
1. home gym budget
2. apartment workout equipment
3. small space fitness
4. beginner home gym
5. no drill home gym
6. workout setup apartment
7. cheap fitness equipment

**Short Description:**
Build a fully functional home gym for under $200 — even if you live in a studio apartment. No drilling, no bulky machines, no clutter. Just smart equipment choices and a workout plan that fits your space.

**Long Description:**
**Stop letting your apartment stop you.**

Most home gym guides assume you have a garage, a basement, or at least a spare room. This one doesn't. *The $200 Home Gym* is built for renters, apartment dwellers, and anyone who can't (or won't) drill into walls or dedicate half their living room to weights.

**Inside you'll find:**
- The exact 8 pieces of equipment that give you 90% of gym functionality for under $200
- A room-by-room layout guide: studio, 1-bedroom, shared space
- Zero-drill storage solutions that keep equipment hidden when not in use
- A 4-week starter workout plan designed for small spaces
- Maintenance and upgrade roadmap: what to buy next when you're ready

**Who this is for:**
Beginners who want to start working out at home without the intimidation of big-box gym equipment or the expense of boutique brands.

**What you need:**
200 square feet, a doorway, and the willingness to move a few things around twice a week.

---

### Step 3 — Manuscript Outline

**Target:** 120 pages, 25,000 words

1. **The Promise** (10 pages) — What you'll have by the end of this book
2. **The Budget Breakdown** (15 pages) — $200 map, where to buy, what to skip
3. **The Equipment Deep Dive** (40 pages) — Each of the 8 pieces: why it matters, what to look for, budget pick vs. upgrade pick
4. **Room Layouts** (20 pages) — Studio, 1-bed, shared living room
5. **The 4-Week Plan** (20 pages) — Full-body workouts, 3x/week, progressive overload
6. **Storage & Maintenance** (10 pages) — Keep it clean, keep it functional
7. **The Upgrade Path** (5 pages) — What to buy when you're ready for more

**Reader Psychology Map:**
- After Chapter 1: "This is possible for me."
- After Chapter 3: "I know exactly what to buy."
- After Chapter 5: "I know exactly what to do."
- After Chapter 7: "I have a system, not just a book."

---

### Step 4 — Cover Design Brief

**Genre Convention:** Photo-realistic, warm lighting, aspirational but achievable. Hero image: clean apartment corner with neatly arranged equipment.

**Dimensions:**
- Paperback: 6×9 in, 300 DPI, bleed 0.125in
- Thumbnail: must read at 80×120px

**Typography:**
- Title: Bold sans-serif, all caps, largest element
- Subtitle: Smaller, sentence case, below title
- Author name: Bottom third, prominent but secondary

**Back Cover:**
- Blurb: 3-4 sentences
- Endorsement: "Finally, a home gym guide that understands apartments." — YouTuber quote
- Barcode/ISBN area

---

### Step 5 — Launch Calendar

**Pre-Launch (Days -14 to -1):**
- Day -14: Email list announcement + cover reveal
- Day -10: ARC outreach to 20 fitness micro-influencers
- Day -7: Social tease: "Something for the apartment crowd"
- Day -3: Pre-order page live
- Day -1: Final reminder email

**Launch Week (Days 0-7):**
- Day 0: Launch email, social push, price at $0.99
- Day 2: YouTube video: "How I Built a $200 Home Gym"
- Day 3: Amazon ad campaign live
- Day 5: Review follow-up to ARC readers
- Day 7: Price raises to $4.99

**Post-Launch (Days 8-21):**
- Day 10: Analyze ad performance, adjust keywords
- Day 14: Newsletter feature: reader home gym photos
- Day 21: Stabilize at $4.99, turn off launch promo
