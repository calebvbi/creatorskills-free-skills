# Newsletter Subscriber Growth Engine

Stop being stuck at 200 subscribers. This free skill gives you a repeatable system for growing your newsletter to 1,000+ — without paid ads, without a big existing audience, and without posting "link in bio" and hoping for the best.

## What It Does

This skill is a five-tool growth system for newsletter writers on Beehiiv, Substack, ConvertKit, or Kit who are stuck in the "friends and family" plateau. It does not teach you how to write better emails — it teaches you how to get more people in the door and keep them there after they arrive.

The five tools:

1. **Growth Audit Diagnostic** — Answers 5 quick questions and spits out your top growth blockers ranked by impact. Tells you exactly what to fix first instead of guessing.
2. **Lead Magnet Concept Generator** — Generates 5 specific lead magnet ideas for your niche with conversion estimates and build times, then picks the strongest one.
3. **Social → Subscriber Conversion Prompts** — Platform-specific CTAs for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok that don't feel like marketing copy.
4. **Cross-Promotion Pitch Template** — A fill-in-the-blank cold email for reaching out to peer creators about newsletter swaps, plus what to track so you know if it worked.
5. **Welcome Sequence First-Impression Optimizer** — Audits your existing welcome email or drafts one from scratch that makes new subscribers think "I'm glad I signed up."

## Who It's For

Newsletter writers who are:
- Stuck under 500 subscribers and want a clear path to 1,000+
- Publishing on Beehiiv, Substack, ConvertKit, or Kit
- Writing about any niche — business, creative, tech, fitness, lifestyle, whatever
- Not willing to spend money on paid ads yet (or ever)
- Tired of advice that only works if you already have 10,000 followers

## How to Install

### Claude Projects

1. Open [Claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and go to **Projects**
2. Create a new project (or use an existing one) for your newsletter
3. Click **Add content** → **Upload files**
4. Upload `SKILL.md`
5. The skill is now active — every conversation in that project treats Claude as your newsletter growth strategist

### ChatGPT

1. Open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) and go to **Explore GPTs** → **Create**
2. In the **Configure** tab, paste the full contents of `SKILL.md` into the **Instructions** field
3. Save and start using it

## Your First Prompt

If you're not sure where to start, run the full audit:

```
I need help growing my newsletter. Here's my situation:
- Subscribers: [number]
- Publishing for: [how long]
- Niche: [what you write about]
- Sending frequency: [how often]
- Current lead magnet: [what it is, or "none"]
- Main traffic source: [social media / word of mouth / my YouTube / etc.]
- Last 3 subjects: [paste them]

Start with the Growth Audit Diagnostic, then help me fix the biggest blocker.
```

## Jump to a Specific Tool

Already know what you need?

**"I need a better lead magnet":**
```
I write a newsletter about [niche] for [target reader]. 
My current lead magnet is [describe it or "none"]. 
Use the Lead Magnet Concept Generator to give me 5 ideas and pick the strongest.
```

**"My social posts aren't getting subscribers":**
```
I'm active on [platforms]. I write about [topic]. 
My newsletter gives subscribers [the main benefit]. 
Generate subscriber CTAs for each of my active platforms.
```

**"I want to do a newsletter swap":**
```
My newsletter: [name], [subscriber count], [niche].
I want to find cross-promo partners in [adjacent niche or same niche].
Write me a cold pitch email and tell me how to track whether the swap worked.
```

**"My welcome email is bad (or I don't have one)":**
```
[Paste your existing welcome email, or say "I don't have one."]
My newsletter: [name, niche, how often I send, lead magnet if any].
Audit it and rewrite it, or draft one from scratch.
```
