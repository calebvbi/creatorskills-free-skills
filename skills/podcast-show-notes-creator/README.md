# Podcast Show Notes Creator

## What It Does

Turns your podcast transcript, outline, or rough notes into complete, SEO-optimized show notes in under 2 minutes. You get an episode summary, timestamped chapters, key quotes, a resource list, and 3 platform-ready social captions — formatted for Apple Podcasts, Spotify, your website, and Substack.

## Who It's For

Podcasters who know they should be writing proper show notes but either skip them (bad for SEO) or spend 30-45 minutes on them each episode. This skill is especially valuable if you have guests — it automatically structures the guest bio, quotes, and resource links.

## Key Features

- **Full show notes package**: summary, timestamps, quotes, guest info, resources, CTAs
- **SEO-optimized**: primary keyword placement, proper header structure, metadata-friendly length
- **3 social captions included**: Instagram, Twitter/X, and LinkedIn — ready to post
- **Works from any input**: transcript, outline, or rough notes
- **Platform-specific formatting tips**: knows the difference between Apple Podcasts and your WordPress site

## Example Use Case

You finish recording a 45-minute interview on "How to Monetize a Podcast Under 1,000 Downloads." You paste the transcript and your guest's name into Claude or ChatGPT with this skill loaded. 2 minutes later you have a 400-word episode summary, 12 timestamped chapters, 3 pull quotes, a guest bio, links to everything mentioned, and social captions for Instagram, Twitter, and LinkedIn.

## How to Install

### Claude Projects
1. Open your Claude project (or create a new one for your podcast)
2. Go to **Project Knowledge** in the left sidebar
3. Upload `SKILL.md`
4. Start a new chat and paste in your transcript or episode outline

### ChatGPT
1. Go to **My GPTs** and click **Create**
2. In the **Configure** tab, paste the contents of `SKILL.md` into the **Instructions** field
3. Name it "Show Notes Creator" and save
4. Upload your transcript directly in the chat

## Your First Prompt

```
I just recorded a podcast episode. Here's the transcript:
[paste transcript]

Episode details:
- Title: "How I Grew My Podcast to 10K Downloads Without Paid Ads"
- Episode number: 42
- Podcast name: The Creator Growth Show
- Primary keyword I want to rank for: "grow podcast without ads"
- CTA: Subscribe on Apple Podcasts

Create the full show notes package.
```

## What You Get Back

1. **Episode summary** (150-250 words, keyword-optimized)
2. **"In This Episode" bullets** (4-6 key takeaways)
3. **Timestamps** (extracted from transcript or placeholders from outline)
4. **Key quotes** (2-3 most shareable moments)
5. **Guest info section** (if applicable)
6. **Resources mentioned**
7. **Show links and CTA**
8. **3 social captions** (Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn)
