# Email Subject Line Optimizer — Quick Start Guide

## What Is This?

The Email Subject Line Optimizer is a free AI skill that turns your email topic into 5 ready-to-send subject line options in seconds. Each option uses a different psychological approach — so instead of agonizing over one subject line, you get a full menu to choose from, complete with a recommendation for which one to use.

Built for newsletter writers, YouTubers, course creators, and anyone who sends emails to an audience they care about.

## How to Install

### Claude (claude.ai)

1. Open [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and go to **Projects**
2. Create a new Project or open an existing one
3. Click **Add content** → **Upload file**
4. Upload `SKILL.md`
5. That's it — the skill is active for every conversation in that Project

### ChatGPT

1. Open [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) and go to **My GPTs** or a custom GPT
2. Paste the contents of `SKILL.md` into the **Instructions** field
3. Save your GPT — the skill is ready to use

### Any other AI (copy-paste method)

1. Open `SKILL.md` and copy the full contents
2. Paste it at the start of any conversation as your first message
3. The AI will follow the skill's instructions for that session

## Your First 5 Minutes

Once installed, try this prompt:

```
My newsletter this week is about how to batch-record YouTube videos in one afternoon.
My audience is part-time creators who struggle to stay consistent.
Generate subject lines for me.
```

You'll get 5 options back — Curiosity Gap, Benefit-Driven, Urgency/Scarcity, Personal, and Question-Based — plus a recommendation.

**That's the whole workflow.** Describe your email, get your options, pick one, send.

## Tips

**Be specific in your input.** The more detail you give about your email's topic and your audience, the more targeted the subject lines will be. One sentence is enough — three sentences is ideal.

**Use the recommendation.** The skill analyzes your email's goal and picks the archetype most likely to perform. Trust it as a starting point, then adjust for your voice.

**Run A/B tests.** The skill always suggests an A/B test pair. If your email platform supports split testing (Beehiiv, ConvertKit, Mailchimp, etc.), test the two options it recommends. You'll learn what your audience responds to faster than any guide can teach you.

**Tell it your tone.** Add a short note like "my newsletter is casual and direct" or "I write in a more professional style" and the subject lines will match your voice much more closely.

**Urgency without a deadline?** If your email doesn't have a real deadline or limited availability, the skill automatically switches the Urgency slot to an "Intrigue / Specificity" option instead — so you always get 5 useful choices regardless of email type.
