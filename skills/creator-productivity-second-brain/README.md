# Creator Productivity Second Brain — Quick Start Guide

Build a productivity system that actually works for how creators operate.

## What Is This?

The Creator Productivity Second Brain designs a personal productivity system built for the reality of creator work: overlapping content cycles, multiple platforms, scattered ideas, and the pull between creation and business.

It connects your long-term goals to today's tasks, captures ideas before they disappear, and builds a weekly rhythm you can sustain.

What you get:
- Connected goal hierarchy (annual → quarterly → monthly → weekly)
- Capture system that works in under 30 seconds
- Time-blocking designed for creative energy
- Weekly review ritual
- Quarterly reflection template

Best for:
- Multi-income creators juggling content, client work, products, and sponsorships
- Creators who feel busy all day but aren't sure if they're working on the right things
- Anyone who's tried productivity systems before and watched them collapse under creator chaos

## Example Use Case

**Scenario:** YouTube channel (90K subs) + consulting. 2 videos/week, 2-3 clients. Best hours: 6-9am, but you spend them on email. 200 Apple Notes and no system. Goals shift weekly.

**What you do:** Open the Second Brain skill. Tell it you need morning blocks for scripting, admin windows for email, and a way to stop losing ideas.

**What you get:** A goal hierarchy: $120K annual breaks into Q2 goals → monthly projects → weekly tasks. Time blocks where 6-9am is sacred deep work, 2-4pm is admin only, and evenings are rest. A capture system using Apple Notes voice memos with a Friday sort ritual. And a 30-minute weekly review.

**Result:** You stop losing ideas. Mornings become your most productive hours. Email stops owning your life. And you finally know why you're doing what you're doing today.

## Pro Tips

- **Build it in under 2 hours.** If it takes longer, you're overcomplicating.
- **The system is alive, not perfect.** Adjust monthly. The goal is a system you *use*, not a flawless plan.
- **Protect deep work at all costs.** Nothing matters if your creative hours get eaten by admin.
