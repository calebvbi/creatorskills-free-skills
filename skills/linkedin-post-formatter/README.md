# LinkedIn Post Formatter

## What it does

This free skill turns rough LinkedIn ideas into polished post drafts with stronger hooks and cleaner formatting. It gives you two versions (story and tactical), plus extra hooks and comment starters.

You keep your idea. The skill fixes structure and readability so the post performs better.

## Who it's for

- Creators building authority on LinkedIn
- Freelancers and consultants using LinkedIn for inbound leads
- Operators who post lessons from real work

## Key features

- Two complete post styles per idea
- Hook variants for quick testing
- Comment starters to increase engagement
- Built-in formatting quality check
- Upgrade path toward monetization outreach

## Example use case

You have a good insight from a client project but your draft reads like a wall of text. Paste your notes and get a clean post format you can publish the same day.

## Starter prompts

- `Turn this idea into a LinkedIn post: "Most creators do not need more tools. They need one repeatable workflow."`
- `Create a story-style and tactical LinkedIn post from this lesson: publishing less often but with clearer positioning brought better leads.`
- `Give me 5 hook options and one complete LinkedIn post for marketers on improving email open rates.`

## How to install

1. Upload `SKILL.md` into Claude Projects or your custom GPT instructions.
2. Paste one rough idea.
3. Pick Version A or B and publish.
